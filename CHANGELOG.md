## What's new

- Smarter arrows: an objective's spawns are grouped into areas, and the arrow points at the nearest
  edge of the nearest real area, ignoring stray spawns far from where a mob really lives
- "Here" depends on what you're heading to: within 10 yards of a turn-in, 20 of an object to gather,
  30 of a place to explore, 40 of mobs
- With Questie installed, arrows also use Questie's spawn locations (setting: "Use Questie's location
  data"), so many more quests get arrows
- Questie: when its quest tracker is on too, a prompt offers to turn it off with one click, and the
  settings window shows its state with a Turn off button
- Right-click a card: "Share quest" shares it with your party; clicked in combat, it's sent when
  combat ends (the game doesn't let addons share in combat)
- Ready quests keep their finished objectives (greyed, with counts) above the turn-in line, so you can
  still tell which quest it is (setting "Keep finished objectives on ready quests", on by default)
- Card layout settings: show or hide the quest name, level, arrow and distance
- Quests with two or more objectives get a progress bar under each objective
- Every quest with a usable item gets a tip (its "Use:" effect, or at least where the item is)
- Items dropped by many similar mobs name the group: "Loot Trogg Stone Tooth from any Stonesplinter"
  instead of listing a dozen mobs

## Fixes

- Card tooltip in a group: party members' progress shows as bars and counts like yours, also for
  objectives without a counter ("Scout through ...") and for members who are ready to turn in
