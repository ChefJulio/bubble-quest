## What's new

- New logo, and an icon in the AddOns list
- Questmarker in the minimap's addon compartment: left-click for settings, right-click to show or
  minimize the cards
- Settings window: tidier and shorter (balanced columns, labels beside their controls, accept types on
  one row, automation options greyed out while automation is off)
- Card tooltip in a group: your progress and each party member's are uniform rows next to each other
  ("You" first), with mini progress bars per objective; item sources moved below them
