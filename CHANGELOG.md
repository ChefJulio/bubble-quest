## What's new

- Right-click a card: "Share quest" shares it with your party; clicked in combat, it's sent when
  combat ends (the game doesn't let addons share in combat)

- Questie: when its quest tracker is on too, a prompt offers to turn it off with one click (Questie's
  own switch; reloads the UI), and the settings window shows its state with a Turn off button

## Fixes

- Card tooltip in a group: party members' progress on objectives without a counter (exploration,
  "Scout through ...") shows as a bar and count like yours, instead of the objective's text
