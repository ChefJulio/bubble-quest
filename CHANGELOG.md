## What's new

- Timed quests show a countdown under the title ("Time left 2:12": orange under a minute, red under
  20 seconds) and in the tooltip, and go to the top of the tracker while the timer runs
- Quests with objectives in different places: a small arrow marks the objective the card's arrow is
  heading for, and the tooltip shows every objective's distance and direction ("180 yd NE")
